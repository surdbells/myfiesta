My tickets

Your tickets live behind the link in your confirmation email.

Open the email

It is from myFiesta, sent the moment payment cleared, and the button in it opens every ticket on the order — QR codes included. Nothing to sign in to. If it is not there, check the spam folder for the address you typed at checkout.

Or check an order

The reference is the short code on your confirmation — it looks like YLQ7J68R. The order page shows what you bought and where the tickets were sent.

Still stuck? Get in touch with the reference, or the name and email from the order.
